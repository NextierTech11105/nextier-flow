Governance · the rules it runs on

  1. A person presses Send. Nothing goes out on its own. One press approves one block, not the next one.
  2. Do Not Call, STOP and wrong numbers are blocked. No call button, no text, from then on. If a check can't run, nothing sends.
  3. Count before you spend. Every pull shows its count and cost first; counting costs 0 credits. Skip trace runs only on lots you approve.
  4. You set the caps. Monthly limits on skip trace and texting. We don't go past them without your yes.
  5. Your keys, your bills. Every vendor bills you directly. You see every line.
  6. Every touch is recorded. Each call, text and email is logged on the lot with the time, the line and the outcome.
  7. One number per conversation. Replies go out from the number that sent the first text, so the owner keeps one thread.
  8. Texting hours. Texts go out between 8 AM and 9 PM ET. We send around 5 PM; replies come in the next morning.
  9. Numbers warm up. A new texting number starts at 250 a day and reaches 500 by day 4. No number is pushed past its daily target.
  10. Your data stays private. Owner names and phones live in your workspace, never on a public page. Only the logins you name get in.
  11. No foreclosure pulls.
  12. Changes are your call. New counties, new signals or more volume get decided in the weekly review.

The first 30 days

Week 1 · Days 1–7

Install

  • Kickoff: counties, targets, who gets a login
  • Keys in: Tracerfy, OneKey, SignalHouse, Follow Up Boss
  • Data lake loaded: NY tax parcels, USBizData NY bundle, Nassau parcel shapes, your MLS
  • Calling numbers bought and set up for calls
  • Texting registration sent to the carriers (their clock; calling doesn't wait for it)
  • First skip trace on the land book: about 1,771 lots, about $35
Done when: you can search any town, open any lot and see the owner and phones.

Done when: calls and replies come in every day.
