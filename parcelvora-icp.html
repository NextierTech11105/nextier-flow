How the phones and emails get filled

USBizData state bundle, cross-referenced against every list above

Business file (5.5M NY): phone, email, website and size for every business segment above. The segment map is written and runs the moment the bundle loads.

Residential 15.8M · cell 5.1M · email 7.3M (NY): matched to investors by owner name at the tax-bill address.

  • R1 the owner's own phone, cell or email
  • R2 someone else in the owner's household has a phone
  • R3 no match, so the owner needs a skip trace
  • R4 company owner, so the match is in the business file or the NYC HPD head officer

Licensees (agents, appraisers, inspectors, contractors) match the business file by business name + ZIP. The Florida bundle is not bought yet.

Rule: no bundle phone is texted or called until line type and Do-Not-Call pass. Parcelvora is a data product.
