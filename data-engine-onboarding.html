How the engine works

1 · Foundational databaseEvery parcel in your state or county from government open data: owner of record, full address, lot size, land use.
2 · State business bundlePeople, cell, email and business records layered onto the same properties.
3 · Scan + tagEvery property gets a deal persona and a deal potential, with the reason and its source.
4 · Owner contactsPhones and emails on your own skip-trace key. Name and address are already on file, so every trace is the low-cost one.
5 · Click to callFrom your own number, on every property. Every outcome logged.

No third-party property data subscription is required. The base is public record, which you own once it is in your database.

The first 30 days

Week 1

Foundation

  • Keys in. Workspace live.
  • Your state's public records cloned into your database, segmented by county.
  • State business bundle layered in.
  • Every property scanned and tagged with a deal persona and deal potential.
Done when: your book opens with every property tagged and every owner of record on file.
Week 2

Reach

  • Your first 10,000 owners traced on your key: phones ranked, line type labeled, Do Not Call checked, known TCPA litigators removed.
  • Your calling number connected.
  • One walkthrough with your team.
Done when: any property opens with its owner and ranked phones, and Call rings from your number.
Week 3

Work it

  • Call blocks of up to 5,000, best properties first.
  • Your team calls from the property. Every outcome logged.
  • Weekly refresh: new listing events tagged and added.
Done when: your team is calling every day and every call is on the record.
Week 4

Read it

  • Scoreboard: sites found · owners reached · consultations booked. Bottom line: cost per deal.
  • Every vendor cost, line by line.
  • Month 2 decided: more counties, more volume, the personas that worked.
Done when: you know what is working and what month 2 looks like.
