The Court Update Robot

It reads every court update the court emails us and writes what happened onto the lead in Zoho. That is the job Jose did by hand.

WHO
  • Built for: EHOA. Thomas, Silvia, and everyone in Group Court Updates.
  • Takes over: Jose's daily court-update work.
  • Where you see it: in Zoho, on the lead, where the team already works. Nobody has to learn anything new.
WHAT

Each time the court sends an update on a foreclosure case, the robot writes three things on that lead in Zoho:

  • the same short note Jose wrote, sent to Group Court Updates
  • the updated Legal Stage
  • the auction date, when one is set

What the robot writes in Zoho

Aged auction means the auction was not held and no new auction is set yet.

It checks every 15 minutes, day and night, weekends included.

WHY
  • Jose cost $400 a week.
  • The court sends about 400 updates a day. One person could get to about 60.
  • About 1 in 10 updates waited more than a month before anyone typed it in.
  • Zoho already saved the full court email. It just threw most of it away, so a person had to look everything up again.
HOW
  1. The court emails an update through eTrack, its own alert service.
  2. Zoho saves the email, the same as always.
  3. Every 15 minutes the robot reads the new emails and works out what changed, the way Jose did.
  4. It writes the note and the stage on the lead, only when something actually changed, so there are no repeat notes.

There's an off switch. Thomas can pause it any time.
