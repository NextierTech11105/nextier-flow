What's inside

One report. Everything recorded.

New York publishes its property records across a dozen portals, each with its own identifiers and delays. Parcelvora joins them into a single record per parcel and shows you where each fact came from.

01

Ownership & deed chain

Current owner of record and every recorded transfer, each linked to its ACRIS document.

02

Mortgages & satisfactions

Open and satisfied mortgages, assignments, recorded lenders and amounts.

03

Liens & judgments

Tax liens, mechanic's liens, judgments and UCC filings where they are recorded.

04

Taxes & assessment

Assessed value, tax class, exemptions and whether the bill is current.

05

Sales history

Prior sales with prices and dates taken from the recorded deeds, not from listings.

06

Building facts

Lot size, building class, units, stories, year built and zoning district.

HPD

Owner contact lookups, only when you ask.

When you need a phone number or email, run a lookup on that owner. You're charged only when a match is found, and every number is screened against the National Do Not Call Registry before it reaches your list.

Contact details come from third-party matching, not from the deed. A match means a likely number, not a verified one. Numbers change hands, and some will be wrong or out of date.

You chooseLookups run per owner, never in bulk by default
Pay on matchNo charge when nothing is found
Best availableLikely matches, never guaranteed accurate

Contact data is for research and lawful outreach. We cannot guarantee any phone number or email is correct or current. DNC flags are applied before export. Parcelvora does not send texts or make calls.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where does the data come from?

Official public records: NYC Open Data (PLUTO, HPD registrations, Department of Finance sales, ACRIS), New York State assessment rolls and tax parcels, and the Florida Department of Revenue's county tax rolls.

Which areas are covered?

Today the lake holds all five NYC boroughs and Nassau and Suffolk on Long Island: 1,888,483 New York parcels. It also holds Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach in Florida. More New York counties come next.

How current is it?

As current as the source. County rolls update once a year, NYC sales every month, and ACRIS deeds run about four weeks behind the county.

Do you have phone numbers and emails?

Not from the public record. Owner contact lookups run only when you ask, you pay only on a match, and every number is screened against the National Do Not Call Registry.

Is contact data guaranteed?

No. A match means a likely number, not a verified one. Numbers change hands, and some will be wrong or out of date.

Does Parcelvora send texts or make calls?

No. Parcelvora is property data, and only data.
