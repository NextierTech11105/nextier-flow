The product

One lookup. The whole ownership story.

Type an address or a parcel number and get the full profile back: the property, the owner, where the owner gets mail, what it's worth and when it last sold.

How Parcelvora works

🔎

1. Search

An address, an NYC lot (BBL), a New York parcel ID or a Florida folio. One box takes all four.

🎛️

2. Filter

Town, ZIP, owner type, absentee, homestead, out of state, value, year built, size, units, sold in the last 12 months.

🗺️

3. Map

The table and the map side by side, always in sync, colored by who owns it.

📄

4. Profile + export

Open any property for its full ownership profile. Download what you filtered as a CSV, up to 5,000 rows at a time.

The data

Built on public record.

Straight from New York City, New York State and the Florida Department of Revenue. Nothing guessed.

NYC Open Data

PLUTO

Every NYC tax lot: owner, building class, units, year built, lot and building area, zoning, assessed value, location.

NYC Open Data

HPD registrations + contacts

Which buildings are registered, and the people on each registration: head officer, owners, officers.

NYC Open Data

DOF rolling sales

Every NYC sale in the last 12 months, with the price and what sold.

NY State Open Data

Local assessment rolls

Owners, mailing address, class, frontage, value, deed and exemptions, on both the 2025 and 2021 rolls.

NY State GIS

Tax parcels

Acres, year built, living area, sewer and water, and the parcel's location on the map.

Florida Dept. of Revenue

County tax rolls

Owner, mailing address, use, market value, homestead, living area, units, land area and the last two sales.
