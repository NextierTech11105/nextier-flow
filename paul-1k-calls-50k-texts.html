🧮 How it's figured

  • Texts ≈ 1¢ each, all-in (0.65¢ to SignalHouse plus the carrier's fee). Calls ≈ 1.15 billed minutes each at 1–2¢ a minute: an estimate until SignalHouse quotes voice.
  • Skip trace is 2¢ a hit on your key; misses cost nothing. Most of it feeds the texts, but phones and emails are data, so the bill sits in the Data Engine.
  • 200 calls a day per caller is a planning number (150–250 on one line). A multi-line dialer would cut the number of callers, but it isn't built yet.
  • 21 weekdays a month. Every call and text is checked against Do Not Call and STOP first. Nothing goes out until a person presses it.
  • Not in these bills: your callers and whoever answers replies (people), and OneKey and Follow Up Boss (your existing accounts). RealEstateAPI isn't needed for this plan.
  • Planning numbers, not a promise. Nextier's install and management fee is quoted by Thomas.
