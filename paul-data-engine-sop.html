The whole job is five steps

Find→Open→Pull→Trace · 2¢→☎ Call

The Data Engine stops at the call. Nothing here ever sends a text blast or a campaign.

1 · Log in

  1. Thomas texts you a link. Tap it, type your name, email, mobile and a password, then press Join and get set up. You only do this once.
  2. Next time, go to nxtier.ai and sign in with that email and password. Same login every time.
  3. You land on your Data Engine. There is no menu to learn. Everything is on this one screen, in the tabs across the top.

3 · Find lots

The list on the left, the map on the right. Owner names show inside your workspace.

  1. Type in the big search box: an address, a town, a ZIP, a parcel ID or an owner's name.
  2. Or tap a filter: Event signal MLS expired Development · zoning Commercial Lot size
  3. Under Development · zoning, tap what you build: Zoning allows a splitBig enough to splitCommercial
  4. Pick a county in the All of New York box. Start with Suffolk, then Nassau. Pick an owner type in Any owner.
  5. The count tells you how many lots match, for example "1,240 of 19,419 lots".
  6. The map follows the list. Click any dot to jump to that lot.

4 · Open a lot

One lot, everything about it, on one page.

  1. Click the 👁 eye or the address on any row.
  2. Read the top: the street photo, the lot's story in bullets, and the loud tags: Aged3 lots by rightAbsentee owner6 developers active here
  3. Read the boxes: LOT · SPLIT · EXPIRED · LAST ASKING · LISTED · OWNER.
  4. The buttons: ☎ Click to call ✉ Email Notes Find similar ▶ Story mode Front of house
  5. Scroll down for the parcel record, Owner & how to reach them, Call + email, the live 3D lot and the live satellite.
  6. ← Property Book takes you back to the list, right where you were.

5 · Get the owner's phone

Above the list there is one bar. It works on the lots in your list, in order, up to 2,000 at a time.

THIS LISTPull into your workspaceTrace · phones + emailsCheck trace resultsDownload this list
  1. Narrow the list to the lots you want to work (one block is up to 2,000).
  2. Press Pull. Those lots become owners in your workspace.
  3. Press Trace. It shows you the cost before it runs: about 2¢ per owner, on your own Tracerfy account.
  4. Give it a few minutes, then press Check trace results.
  5. Every phone lands on its lot with its line type (mobile or landline) and a DNC flag if it has one.
  6. Just one lot? Open it and press Trace this one.

6 · Call

  1. Open a traced lot. The Call + email card lists every phone with its line type.
  2. Press ☎ Call. It rings from your SignalHouse.io line.
  3. The call and how it went are logged on that owner, so the next call starts where this one ended.
  4. Skip any number marked DNC.

Your SignalHouse line and Tracerfy account are connected at install. Until they are, Call and Trace tell you what is missing.

Coming next

💬 Click to text, right next to Call

One text to one owner from your own line. A message library sorted by stage (Opener, Follow-up, Appointment, Reply): tap one and it drops in with the owner's first name and street already filled. When they write back, the reply pops up on your screen. One text at a time, never a campaign.

7 · Property Search: all of New York

  1. Open the Data Engine · Property Search tab.
  2. Pick a county (Suffolk and Nassau are at the top), a property type (Vacant land, 1-family homes, Commercial …) and a lot size. Tap ½ acre + or 1 acre +. Press Search.
  3. Read each owner line. It says who owns it and where they get their mail, in plain words: Trust · mails out of state (FL) Individual · lives there
  4. Most likely first puts estates, out-of-state owners and absentee owners on top.
  5. Group by town or school district (zoning district in NYC). Tap a group to narrow to it.
  6. Radius: type an address in Near, pick the miles, press Search. Or switch to Map and click any spot.
  7. Find: type a parcel ID, an address or an owner's name.
  8. Full report opens the whole public record. In NYC every deed and mortgage opens the scanned document.
  9. ⬇ CSV blocks downloads the results in blocks of 2,000, ranked and labeled. Block 01 holds the most likely sellers, ready for a 2¢ skip trace.

Territory Intelligence & News Feed

Your territory at a glance. The tabs across its top (Scan · Funnel · Segments · Market · Where) each tell one part of the story.

Your first 10 minutes

  1. Open Data Engine. Set the county box to Suffolk.
  2. Under Development · zoning, tap Zoning allows a split.
  3. Click the #1 lot. Read the tags, then press ▶ Story mode and let it walk you through.
  4. Press ← Property Book. Press Pull, then Trace. Check the cost, then confirm.
  5. A few minutes later: Check trace results. Open the lot again and press ☎ Call.
  6. Then try Property Search: Suffolk → Vacant land → 1 acre + → Near your office → 2 mi → Search. Tap Map, then ⬇ CSV blocks.

The rules

  • One click, one call.Nothing in the Data Engine sends in bulk. Ever.
  • DNC and STOP are respected.A number marked DNC is never called. A STOP is never texted again.
  • Your accounts, your costs.Traces run on your Tracerfy account (about 2¢ an owner). Calls ring from your SignalHouse line.
  • The public record is the source.Owners, mailing addresses, lot sizes and zoning come from New York's own records.
