Parcelavora — One-Page Plan

October 27, 2026

The idea

Parcelavora sells New York property data, and only data, at a flat monthly price far below the four tools investors and agents pay for today. The core is public record: NYC Open Data and NY State Open Data hold 3,441 tables and about 8.9 billion rows, and pulling them costs $0.

We take the best screen from each tool and rebuild it on that data: PropertyShark's property report, PropStream's list builder, DealMachine's Driving for Dollars map and ATTOM's one clean record per property. At launch we charge extra only where we pay: phone and email lookups.

No texting, dialing or mail. The customer gets the data and works it their own way.

Risks and limits

The biggest limits are coverage and freshness, not cost.

  • New York only at launch. Other states need paid data (RealEstateAPI or ATTOM). Price that as an add-on so the $29 plan stays cheap to run.
  • Staten Island has no deed history. ACRIS covers Manhattan, Bronx, Brooklyn and Queens only.
  • Some feeds run late. The newest deed in ACRIS open data is from 2026-09-30. The tax lien list was last updated 330 days ago, HPD registrations 76 days ago. Show an as-of date on every fact.
  • Contacts carry a compliance duty. Customers who text or call need Do Not Call checks. Tracerfy offers one; include the DNC flag on every number we sell.
  • Unlimited invites scraping. Cap exports per month so nobody resells the whole book.
  • Confirm reuse terms. Check that the NYC and NY State open-data terms allow commercial resale before launch.
  • Separate company, separate keys. Its own Tracerfy, RealEstateAPI and Stripe accounts. Never EHOA's or Nextier's.
  • Remix ideas, not property. Build our own screens. Copy no competitor's code, wording or branding.
