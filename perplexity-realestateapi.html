Set it up · 5 minutes · needs Perplexity Pro or Max

  1. In your RealEstateAPI dashboard, copy an API key. Use one that's separate from EHOA's daily key.
  2. In Perplexity, go to Settings → Connectors → + Custom connector and pick Remote.
  3. Name it RealEstateAPI and paste this address:
https://mcp.realestateapi.com/sse
  1. If the form lets you add a header, name it x-api-key and paste your key as its value.
  2. If it doesn't, choose no authentication and use this address instead, with your key in place of YOUR_KEY:
https://mcp.realestateapi.com/sse?x-api-key=YOUR_KEY

Done when Perplexity answers "Who owns" a real address with an owner's name.

Costs and safety

  • Every lookup spends your RealEstateAPI credits. Check your usage after the first 5 questions. Connectors can bill more than direct calls.
  • Skip traces cost the most. Ask for phones only when you need them.
  • Your key works like a password. If it ever leaks, make a new one in your RealEstateAPI dashboard.
