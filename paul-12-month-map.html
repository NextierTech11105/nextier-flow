Every text is checked against STOP and Do Not Call first. Follow-ups stop when an owner replies or says STOP.

Each number can take up to 2,000 texts a day (the carrier's limit). We run them at 500 so the carriers don't start filtering. More volume means more numbers, never pushing one number harder.

These are planning numbers, not a promise. The simulator assumes 5,000 new owners a month, which comes to about $31K in a normal month. This plan sends 12,000 new owners a month plus their follow-ups. Run the simulator again once month 5's real numbers are in.
