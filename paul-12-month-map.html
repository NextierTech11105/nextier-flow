50,000 a month by calls alone takes about 12 callers.

Both: text first, then your callers call everyone who got the text but didn't reply. Or call first, then text the owners you couldn't reach. Nothing goes out until a person presses it.

How 50,000 adds up

  • ~40,000first texts. Wednesday to Saturday, Send pressed at 5 PM, about 2,500 an evening. One press runs until the evening is done.
  • ~10,000follow-ups. 500 every weekday, to owners who didn't answer, 30+ days after their first text.

Why evenings: people reply when they wake up. Thursday to Sunday mornings are for working replies.

How the calls add up

  • ~200calls a day per caller, with click to call.
  • ~4,000calls a month per caller (21 weekdays).
  • ~12,000a month with 3 callers. The year below uses 3 callers.

Each caller you add: about 4,000 more calls a month.

Every call and text is checked against Do Not Call and STOP first. Follow-up texts stop when an owner replies or says STOP.

Each texting number can take up to 2,000 texts a day (the carrier's limit). We run them at 500 so the carriers don't start filtering. More texts means more numbers, never pushing one number harder.

Holidays are off: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, the Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.

These are planning numbers, not a promise. Calls assume about 200 a day per caller with click to call. The simulator assumes 5,000 new owners a month, which comes to about $31K in a normal month. Run it again once real numbers are in.
