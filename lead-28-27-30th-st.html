How to reach the owner

A sale decision sits with Verizon's corporate real estate group, not with anyone at the building. These are the doors in, best first.

  1. Cushman & Wakefield · Josh Kuriloff's teamThe brokerage team Verizon used to sell part of 140 West St to a developer.
  2. Ethan Kannry · VerizonNamed as the owner's representative on the building's most recent city permit filing (April 2026). No phone is listed in public filings.
  3. Verizon corporate real estateNo public contact page. Reach it through either of the two above.

C T Corporation System at 28 Liberty St is Verizon's registered agent for legal papers only. It is not a sales contact.

How Verizon has sold before

140 West St, Manhattan
$274M · ~40% of the building
22 floors sold as a condo interest to Magnum Real Estate. Verizon kept the switching floors.
435 W 50th St, Manhattan
$20M · 101,000 of 300,000 sq ft
Sold to JDS Development and Property Markets Group. Verizon kept the rest.

The offer that fits: buy the unused building rights or the floors Verizon doesn't need, and leave the switching equipment in place. That is the shape of both past deals.
