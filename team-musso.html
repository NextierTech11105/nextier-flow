Chapter 1 of 13

You asked. Straight answers.

Everything your team raised, answered once, plainly.

"Can Claude and chat in our Follow Up Boss help with lead gen?"

Chat inside a CRM talks to the leads you already have. It doesn't find a single new one. Nextier finds them, starts the conversation, and hands Follow Up Boss only the owners worth a call.

"Can our guy work inside Claude with you?"

Yes. Claude with no data is a brilliant intern with an empty desk. Connect it to your own data engine and it builds a list from one sentence.

"Pull every commercial expired from the last two years."

That's one sentence, not a week of exports and a spreadsheet nobody trusts. Tagged, traced and sitting in your Property Book before the coffee's cold.

"We want to send mail."

Send it. You'll have the owner's real mailing address, absentee owners included. Just know a postcard has never texted anyone back. So the mail goes out with the text, not instead of it.

"We want to send email for lead gen."

First you need their email address, which a cold list doesn't have. We trace it. Then email goes out from the same list that already texted them, so nobody gets pitched by three tools that never talk to each other.

"Lead gen is what's missing."

Agreed. It's the only thing this was built to do. Everything after the hand-raise is already your team's strength.

"Just put everything in the CRM."

That's how CRMs die. Forty thousand rows, thirty-five thousand who never answer, and your best agent scrolling past all of them. Follow Up Boss is for conversations. Nextier keeps the cold out.

"Or we hire a developer."

A developer will bill you to build what's already running, then bill you again when it breaks. This is installed in your own account, on your own keys, and it already works.
