What it does

  1. Finds the land. Every parcel in New York: state tax rolls, the NYC lot file, OneKey MLS.
  2. Finds the reason to call now. Expired and never relisted, expired twice, long on market, estate, developers buying next door.
  3. Reads the deal. Acres, lots by right, last asking, who's building nearby, permit trend.
  4. Finds the owner. Owner of record, past the LLC to the person who signs.
  5. Gets phones and emails. Skip trace on your key: every phone tagged mobile or landline, with the Do Not Call check.
  6. Calls and emails from your line. One click to call, one press to log the outcome, notes on every lot.
  7. Pushes to outreach. First text or nurture. Nothing sends until a person presses Send.
  8. Books into your CRM. Appointments go to Follow Up Boss.

Capacity

  • Texting: up to 500 a day per number once warmed (250 → 400 → 500 over 4 days). Five numbers ≈ 2,500 a day.
  • Send window: 8 AM–9 PM ET. Sends go at 5 PM; replies land the next morning.
  • Calling: one line at a time today, from your own numbers. 500 calls a day ≈ $6–12 a day in minutes (estimate; the carrier quotes voice). One caller makes about 150–250 dials a day; spread calls across 5–10 numbers.
  • Skip trace: about 2¢ a lookup on your key. The whole land book ≈ $35.
  • Numbers: about $1.15 a month each.
