What it does

  1. Finds the land. Every parcel in New York: state tax rolls, the NYC lot file, OneKey MLS.
  2. Finds why now. Expired and never relisted, expired twice, long on market, estate, developers buying next door.
  3. Reads the deal. Acres, lots by right, last asking, who's building nearby, permit trend.
  4. Finds the owner. Owner of record, past the LLC to the person who signs.
  5. Gets phones and emails. Skip trace on your key: every phone tagged mobile or landline, with the Do Not Call check.
  6. Puts it on one record. Land, listing, owner, person and contacts joined on every lot.
  7. Watches it every week. Suffolk and Nassau swept for new expireds, back on the market, new deeds and permits.

Capacity

  • Coverage: every parcel in New York State. Heavy focus on Suffolk and Nassau.
