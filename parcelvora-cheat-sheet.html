Parcelvora Cheat Sheet

Everything about Parcelvora in one place. Updated October 28, 2026.

What it is

  • Parcelvora sells New York property data, and only data. Silvia's company. It is not named Nextier.
  • It takes the best of four tools: PropertyShark's report, PropStream's list builder, DealMachine's map and ATTOM Property Navigator's comps and values.
  • Most of the data is free public record (NYC + NY State Open Data). Paid only for phones, home values and foreclosures.
  • Domains parcelvora.com, .ai and .net were registered Oct 27 through Squarespace. The US trademark office has no filings for PARCELVORA.

Rules to remember

  • Share links like this page (public, no login). Claude links make people log in.
  • Never fork or share the Nextier repo. Its history holds saved keys and every client's code. Copy only the pieces Parcelvora needs.
  • The old repo named nextier is a full platform copy with saved keys. Keep it private.
  • Parcelvora uses its own keys: Tracerfy, RealEstateAPI, Stripe. Never EHOA's or Nextier's.
  • A key in a connector address works like a password. If it leaks, make a new one.
  • Connectors can bill more than direct calls. Check credits after the first few lookups.

Data limits

  • NY open data has no foreclosure or lis pendens filings.
  • Staten Island deeds are not in ACRIS. ACRIS open data runs about 4 weeks behind.
  • Some feeds are stale: tax lien list (330 days), HPD registrations (76 days).
  • 177,081 NYC owners have no first/last split (no comma in the record). The full name still shows.
  • No phones or emails in public data. That's the paid skip-trace step.
