  9. Layer in the Deal Origination Engine. The first text starts the conversation. Every reply is classified, prioritized and routed to the right closer. Conversations compound into deal flow.
  10. NXTPOST feeds the engine. NXTPOST floods social, search and AI answers with your market's story. Comments, DMs and clicks come back as intel and event signals once the feed is set up at install.

The core engine

Observe. Detect. Classify. Prioritize. Execute. Learn.

The Data Engine powers the initial campaign engine, and this loop runs under every campaign. The communication rails are fast, intentional and two-way: texts go out, replies and calls come back, and every touch makes the next one sharper. That's how it drives revenue at a much lower cost and gives your people their bandwidth back.

  1. 01Observe

    Every send, reply, call, note and disposition is on the record.

  2. 02Detect

    New signals, hand raises and callbacks surface the moment they happen.

  3. 03Classify

    Every reply is sorted by intent: positive, question, later or opt-out.

  4. 04Prioritize

    The hottest conversations jump to the top of your team's board.

  5. 05Execute

    A person presses launch, call or book. Texts go out, calls come in.

  6. 06Learn

    Every month we review dispositions, notes and messaging against the numbers: texts out vs positive replies in. Then the next month gets sharper.
